# Video shotlist: Erosion Maze (under 60 s)

Record on a phone (or a phone-sized viewport) in the public test subreddit, against real posts. No staged data: the erosion shots need real play, so schedule recording for an Evolved_Day (Tuesday to Sunday) that follows a day on which real players finished runs. If the day before had no trails, the Launch_Screen honestly shows that; do not fake traffic.

| # | Sec | Shot | Notes |
|---|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | 0-5 | Scroll the feed, stop on today's post | The maze, yesterday's Heatmap and the "Day N · Hook" title must be readable. Narrate: "One maze a day, and yesterday's players shaped it." |
| 2 | 5-12 | Hold on the Launch_Screen | Point at the Community_Route line and the worn path. Narrate: "Paths the crowd walked wore open overnight; untouched corners grew into hedges." |
| 3 | 12-15 | Tap Play; the expanded game opens | The ghost-hand hint shows the first swipe, no tutorial text. |
| 4 | 15-30 | Solve with real swipes | Step through one hedge so the cost-2 move is visible, and bump one wall (costs 0). Keep the cost, Par and Move_Budget readout in frame. |
| 5 | 30-40 | The End_Card | Cost against Par, "N trails found today", the Distribution and Percentile only if the Field has 2 or more Solved, streak block, UTC countdown. Narrate only the numbers on screen. |
| 6 | 40-47 | Tap "Comment my result" | Cut to the posted comment under the stickied Results_Thread: the spoilered Share_Grid. Explain that it shows which regions you crossed, not the route. |
| 7 | 47-54 | Leaderboard rows with snoovatars; Streak_Flair toggle; Trailblazer credit if earned | Mention flair is opt-in and every Reddit action is a separate tap. |
| 8 | 54-60 | Side by side: yesterday's closed post and today's board | Title card: "Erosion Maze: the board remembers the crowd." |

Recording checklist:

- [ ] Public test subreddit (fewer than 200 members), light mode, plus a dark-mode cutaway in shot 5 if time allows
- [ ] Sound off by default, then one clip with sound on for shot 4
- [ ] No console open, no personal info, no invented numbers anywhere
- [ ] Real taps only: comment, join and flair each shown as an explicit tap
- [ ] Shot 8 uses two real consecutive days; if the previous day was a Seed_Day boundary, pick another pair
