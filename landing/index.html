The problem

Daily puzzles forget everyone at midnight.

A daily puzzle is a shared ritual, but each day starts from a blank slate. Whatever the crowd did yesterday is gone. Playing together really means playing alone, then comparing notes afterwards.

  • A blank slate every dayThe board resets at midnight. Nothing you did yesterday carries into today.
  • The community only meets in the commentsPlayers talk about the puzzle, but they never touch the same board. The puzzle itself has no memory of them.
  • Your run changes nothing for anyone elseA great solve or a clever shortcut is a score on a list. It does not shape what the next player sees.
The solution

Make the crowd part of the puzzle.

In Erosion Maze, the board is the shared space. Paths people walk wear open. Corners nobody visits grow over. Today's maze carries yesterday's footsteps.

How a day works

One post, one maze, one scored run.

  1. Open today's post

    The feed preview already shows today's maze, yesterday's heatmap and the community route, before you tap anything.

  2. Find your way

    Move from the ring to the arch with swipes, a direction pad or the keyboard. Finish within the day's move budget. Lowest cost wins.

  3. One scored attempt

    Logged-in players get one scored attempt per day. Everything after that is practice, so you can explore freely.

  4. See where you stand

    The end card shows your cost against par, the leaderboard, your streak, and a spoiler-safe result you can share as a comment. The first player to find a new route gets Trailblazer credit.

The weekly arc

A maze that changes all week.

Monday brings a new maze. Tuesday to Sunday, each day grows out of the one before, so the crowd decides which shortcuts exist tomorrow. At the same time the move budget tightens, so the week gets harder as the board opens up.

Always solvable

Every maze, fresh or eroded, must pass the same check: it can be solved, par is proven by a solver, and the budget sits above par. Erosion can never wall anyone in.

Scores you can trust

Your device sends only your moves. The server replays them with the same rules and works out the cost itself, so a result can't be faked.

Deterministic, not generated

The daily maze comes from a seeded generator and a fixed erosion rule. No AI writes the puzzle, no paid services, no outside requests.
